FAQ

Why does the app ask for location?
To calculate Fajr and Asr times where you are and remind you after them. It's computed on your device, and you can pick your city manually in Settings instead.

I don't get reminders.
Allow notifications in iOS Settings → Adhkar → Notifications, and make sure reminders are on in the app's settings.

Does it work offline?
Yes, the whole app works offline.
